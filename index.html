Important contribution information:

  • Only individuals who are residents of Manitoba may contribute.
  • Contributions must be made using the contributor's own funds.
  • Contributions under $25 are not eligible for the City rebate.
  • Campaign contributions are not charitable donations and are not income-tax deductible.
  • To receive a rebate, the contributor must apply to the City Clerk within 14 months after election day and provide the campaign contribution receipt.
  • Rebate eligibility and payment are administered by the City of Winnipeg and remain subject to applicable law, the City's rebate program, and the campaign's required financial filings.

Rebate figures are estimates based on the City of Winnipeg's current rebate formula.
